# PreFlight Insight 技术支持

如遇到放行包无法导入、内容显示异常或其他使用问题，请发邮件至 **[preflight.insight@qq.com](mailto:preflight.insight@qq.com)**。

为便于排查，请在邮件中说明 iPad 型号、iPadOS 版本、App 版本，以及问题发生前后的操作步骤。请勿发送未经脱敏的真实放行包、航班号、航路、乘客资料或其他敏感运行信息。

## Privacy

For help with importing a briefing package or other app issues, email **[preflight.insight@qq.com](mailto:preflight.insight@qq.com)**. Include your iPad model, iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not email unredacted briefing packages, flight numbers, routes, passenger information, or other sensitive operational data.
