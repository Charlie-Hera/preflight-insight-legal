# PreFlight Insight 隐私政策

**生效日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日**

**最后更新：2026 年 10 月 2 日**

## 一、适用范围

PreFlight Insight（“本 App”）是面向 iPad 的放行包阅读与信息整理工具。本政策描述当前版本如何处理信息。当前版本不提供账号注册或登录，因此不会通过 App 收集用于注册账号的手机号、邮箱或其他账户资料。若未来版本新增账号功能并收集个人信息，开发者将在该版本发布前按实际处理方式更新本政策和 App Store Connect 隐私披露。

## 二、信息如何处理

**导入的放行包和解析结果：** 用户选择导入的 `.efbFPP` 文件、其中的 PDF 附件及解析结果在 iPad 本机处理，并保存在 App 的本机档案中供查看和重新打开。本 App 不会把这些文件或解析结果上传到开发者运营的服务器。

**相机：** 用户主动打开 FMC 航路核对并授权相机后，App 使用相机实时识别画面中的文字。图像帧只在设备内存中处理；App 不录制视频、不保存图像，也不把图像或识别结果发送到服务器。用户可在 iPad 设置中管理相机权限。

**运行日志：** App 会向 iPadOS 系统日志写入解析性能信息。部分日志可能包含航路代码、巡航高度层、计划起飞/到达时间、跑道提示和航路点数量。App 不会主动把这些日志上传给开发者。若用户主动分享系统诊断日志或 Xcode 控制台内容，其中可能包含上述飞行运行信息；分享前请检查并遮盖敏感内容。

**技术支持邮件：** App 不会自动读取用户的邮箱或通讯录。若用户主动发邮件至 `preflight.insight@qq.com`，开发者会收到发件邮箱地址和邮件内容，并将其用于回复和处理该项咨询。请勿发送未经脱敏的真实放行包、航班号、航路或乘客资料。

## 三、本机保存、删除与备份

用户可在 App 的历史档案中查看并删除已保存的放行包档案。App 会将其档案数据库和缓存设置为排除系统设备备份。此设置仅适用于 App 沙盒内的数据，不控制用户另行导出或保存到“文件”等位置的副本，也不会移除该设置生效前已创建的备份。卸载 App 后，iPadOS 会清理 App 本机数据。

## 四、第三方组件与网络

当前版本未集成广告、统计或埋点 SDK。本 App 不会将导入文件、解析结果、相机画面或识别结果发送给开发者运营的服务器。用户通过系统功能主动发送邮件或打开外部链接时，相关内容由用户选择的系统 App 或服务处理，并适用相应服务自身的政策。

## 五、儿童隐私

本 App 面向成年专业人士，不以儿童为目标用户，也不要求儿童提供个人信息。

## 六、政策变更

若 App 的信息处理方式发生变化，开发者会更新本政策的“最后更新”日期，并在相应版本发布前更新 App Store Connect 中的隐私披露。

## 七、使用范围说明

本 App 用于呈现和整理放行包资料，输出仅供参考，不提供运行决策建议，也不替代飞行员判断、公司程序、官方 EFB 或签派放行。

## 八、联系方式

如对本政策或个人信息处理有疑问，请联系：**[preflight.insight@qq.com](mailto:preflight.insight@qq.com)**

# PreFlight Insight Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 2, 2026**

**Last updated: October 2, 2026**

## 1. Scope

PreFlight Insight (the “App”) is an iPad app for viewing and organizing flight briefing packages. This Policy describes the current version. The current version does not offer account registration or sign-in and does not collect a phone number, email address, or other account details through the App for account creation. If a future version adds accounts and collects personal information, the developer will update this Policy and the App Store Connect privacy disclosures to reflect the actual practices before that version is released.

## 2. How information is handled

**Briefing packages and parsed results:** `.efbFPP` files selected by the user, their PDF attachments, and parsed results are processed on the iPad and stored in the App’s local archive for viewing and reopening. The App does not upload these files or results to servers operated by the developer.

**Camera:** When the user opens FMC route checking and grants camera access, the App uses the camera to recognize text in real time. Image frames are processed in device memory; the App does not record video, save images, or send images or recognition results to a server. Camera access can be managed in iPad Settings.

**Operational logs:** The App writes parsing-performance information to the iPadOS system log. Some entries may include route codes, cruise flight levels, planned departure/arrival times, runway hints, and waypoint counts. The App does not actively upload these logs to the developer. If the user chooses to share system diagnostic logs or Xcode console output, those materials may contain the operational information above; review and redact sensitive details before sharing.

**Support email:** The App does not automatically access the user’s email or contacts. If the user voluntarily emails `preflight.insight@qq.com`, the developer receives the sender’s email address and message and uses them to respond to and handle that inquiry. Do not send unredacted briefing packages, flight numbers, routes, or passenger information.

## 3. Local storage, deletion, and backups

Users can view and delete saved briefing-package archives in the App’s archive. The App marks its archive database and cache to be excluded from system device backups. This setting applies only to data in the App sandbox; it does not control copies that users export or save elsewhere, such as in Files, and does not remove backups created before the setting took effect. When the App is uninstalled, iPadOS removes its local App data.

## 4. Third-party components and network activity

The current version does not include advertising, analytics, or telemetry SDKs. The App does not send imported files, parsed results, camera frames, or recognition results to servers operated by the developer. If a user chooses to send an email or open an external link using a system feature, the selected system app or service handles that activity under its own policies.

## 5. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for adult professionals, is not directed at children, and does not ask children to provide personal information.

## 6. Changes to this Policy

If the App’s information practices change, the developer will update the “Last updated” date and revise the App Store Connect privacy disclosures before releasing the relevant version.

## 7. Intended use

The App presents and organizes briefing-package information for reference. It does not provide operational decision recommendations and does not replace pilot judgment, company procedures, an official EFB, or dispatch release.

## 8. Contact

For questions about this Policy or information handling, contact **[preflight.insight@qq.com](mailto:preflight.insight@qq.com)**.
